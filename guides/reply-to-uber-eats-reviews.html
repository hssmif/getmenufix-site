How to Reply to Uber Eats Reviews (With Examples)

Reviews can sting, especially when the problem was the delivery, not your food. A calm, short reply and a quick fix to the cause go a long way.

Updated 10 October 2026 · By Abdel Iflillis, founder of MenuFix

Quick answer

Reply to Uber Eats reviews briefly, politely and personally: thank the customer, acknowledge any problem without arguing, say what you've done about it, and keep it short. Then fix the cause on your menu or in the kitchen, so the same complaint doesn't come back.

In this guide
  1. Read every review
  2. Keep replies short and personal
  3. Thank people for good reviews
  4. For a complaint, own it and stay calm
  5. Never share private details
  6. Fix the cause
  7. Report reviews that break the rules

1. Read every review

Look at your feedback in Uber Eats Manager regularly. One comment is one person's view; the same comment twice is a pattern worth acting on.

2. Keep replies short and personal

Two or three sentences is plenty. Use the customer's first name if it shows, mention the dish, and sign off with your own name. A copied and pasted reply on every review reads as if nobody is listening.

3. Thank people for good reviews

A quick thank you is enough:

"Thanks so much, Priya! Glad the laksa hit the spot. See you next time. Minh, Pho Corner"

4. For a complaint, own it and stay calm

Don't argue, even when you're sure you're right. Acknowledge the problem, say what you've done, and leave the door open:

"Sorry your chips arrived soggy, Tom. We've changed to vented boxes so they stay crisp on the way. We'd love another chance. Sam"

If the issue was the delivery, you can say so gently without blaming anyone: "Sorry it arrived late. We'll pass this on, and we always pack hot food to travel as well as possible."

5. Never share private details

Don't mention order numbers, addresses or anything personal in a public reply. If something needs sorting out, follow the refund and support options in Uber Eats Manager.

6. Fix the cause

Many complaints point to something on your page:

  • "Not what I expected": rewrite the description so it says what's in the dish and how big it is.
  • "Smaller than the photo": retake the photo so it shows a real serving.
  • "Missing item": check that combos and options say exactly what's included.
  • "Cold" or "soggy": look at packaging, and consider taking dishes that don't travel well off delivery.

Our guide to writing menu descriptions helps with the first two.

7. Report reviews that break the rules

Abusive or offensive reviews can be reported through Uber Eats Manager or support. Honest negative feedback usually stays, and that's where a good reply and a real fix matter most.

Questions

Can I reply to reviews on Uber Eats?

Uber Eats Manager lets restaurants respond to customer feedback, depending on your account and region. Look under Feedback or Reviews in Uber Eats Manager.

Should I reply to bad reviews?

Yes, when you can do it calmly. A short, polite reply that owns the problem shows other customers that you care. Never argue or share private details.

Can I remove a bad review on Uber Eats?

Generally no. If a review breaks Uber Eats rules, for example abusive language, you can report it through Uber Eats Manager or support. Otherwise, the best response is to fix the cause.
