How to Organise Your Uber Eats Menu Categories

A clear menu structure helps hungry people find what they want quickly. Here is how to set up your categories so your page is easy to scan on a phone.

Updated 10 October 2026 · By Abdel Iflillis, founder of MenuFix

Quick answer

Organise your Uber Eats menu into a small number of clear categories in the order people eat: a best sellers category first, then mains or your core dishes, then sides, drinks and desserts. Use plain names, avoid categories with one item, and remove anything empty or duplicated.

1. Start with a best sellers category

Create a first category called Most Popular, Best Sellers or House Favourites and put your five or six most ordered dishes in it. Many people never scroll far, so this is where your best food should be.

2. Follow the order people eat

After your best sellers, list the dishes most people come for, then the rest. A typical order is:

  1. Best sellers
  2. Your main dishes, split by type if you have many (Burgers, Wraps, Bowls)
  3. Sides and extras
  4. Drinks
  5. Desserts

A café might use Breakfast, Lunch, Coffee, Cold drinks and Cakes instead. Group things the way your customers think about them.

3. Use plain names

Category names are signposts. Noodle Soups is quicker to understand than "From the Pot". If you like a creative name, add the plain word: Nonna's Kitchen: Pasta.

4. Not too many, not too few

Lots of tiny categories make people scroll through headings instead of food. One huge category hides your best dishes. Aim for categories with a handful of items each, and merge any that only have one or two.

5. Remove what's empty, old or duplicated

Old categories from a previous menu, empty ones and near duplicates ("Drinks" and "Beverages") make a page look unloved. Take five minutes to clean them up in Uber Eats Manager.

6. Put the best item first in each category

The order inside a category matters too. Lead each category with its most popular or most photogenic dish, and keep items with photos near the top.

7. Use time based menus if you have them

If you serve breakfast and dinner, Uber Eats lets you set menus for different times of day. That way customers only see what you can actually make at that hour.

Questions

How should I order my categories on Uber Eats?

Put a best sellers category first, then the dishes most people come for, then sides, extras, drinks and desserts at the end.

Can an item be in more than one category?

Yes, Uber Eats Manager lets you place the same item in more than one category. It is handy for putting best sellers at the top without removing them from their usual place.

What should I name my categories?

Use words customers already use: Burgers, Pizzas, Noodles, Sides, Drinks. Creative names are fine for your shop, but plain names are faster to scan on a phone.
