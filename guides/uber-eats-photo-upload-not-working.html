Uber Eats Photo Upload Not Working? Common Problems and Fixes for Restaurants

You've taken a good photo, but Uber Eats Manager won't take it, or it never appears on your page. This guide is for restaurant owners and goes through the usual causes one by one, based on Uber's own photo rules.

Updated 10 October 2026 · By Abdel Iflillis, founder of MenuFix

Quick answer

When an Uber Eats photo won't upload or gets rejected, the cause is usually one of these: the file is bigger than 10 MB, it's in a format Uber doesn't accept (like HEIC from an iPhone), it's smaller than 550 × 440 pixels or the wrong shape, or it has text, logos or more than one item on it. Fix the file, then upload it again in Uber Eats Manager.

1. The file is too big

Uber's menu photo guidelines allow files up to 10 MB. Photos straight from a new phone can be larger.

Fix: export the photo again at a smaller size or a little more compression. At 2880 × 2304 pixels a JPG is normally well under the limit.

2. The format isn't accepted

Uber accepts JPG, and also PNG and GIF for menu items. iPhones often save photos as HEIC, and some apps save as WEBP. Those aren't on Uber's list.

Fix: save or share the photo as a JPG. On an iPhone you can set the camera to save JPGs: Settings, then Camera, then Formats, then Most Compatible.

3. The photo is too small or the wrong shape

Uber needs at least 550 pixels wide and 440 pixels tall, and recommends 2880 × 2304 pixels, a 5:4 landscape shape. A screenshot, a photo saved from a chat app or a tall portrait photo often fails here.

Fix: use the original photo, not a copy from WhatsApp or Messenger, and crop it to 5:4 with the dish in the middle. The Uber Eats photo size guide has every size in one table.

4. It uploads, then gets rejected

Every photo is reviewed. According to Uber's guidelines, photos are turned down for things like:

  • Text, prices, discounts or banners on the image.
  • Logos or watermarks.
  • More than one menu item, unless they're sold together.
  • People or faces (hands holding the food are fine).
  • Blurry, dark or very small photos.
  • Stock photos, or photos that aren't your dish.

Fix: retake the photo of your real dish, on its own, with nothing added on top. Our guide to food photos on your phone helps.

5. The cover image doesn't look right

The cover image at the top of your store has its own rules: 2880 × 2304 pixels, 5:4, JPG, filling the whole space with no empty margins, and no logos or text apart from your store name (Uber's cover image guide).

Fix: crop a landscape food photo to exactly that size. See choosing your banner photo.

6. You uploaded it, but it isn't showing yet

Because photos are reviewed, they can take longer to appear than a name or price change.

Fix: wait, then check the item again in Uber Eats Manager before uploading a second copy.

7. The upload button does nothing

This is usually the browser, not the photo.

Fix: use Uber Eats Manager on a computer, update your browser or try another one, and reload the page. If it still fails, contact Uber Eats support from Uber Eats Manager. Our guide to editing your menu in Uber Eats Manager shows where photos are added.

Quick checklist before you upload

  • JPG, under 10 MB.
  • 2880 × 2304 pixels, or at least 550 × 440, in a 5:4 shape.
  • One real dish, centred, nothing written on it.
  • Sharp and bright.

Questions

Why won't my photo upload to Uber Eats?

Check the file first: under 10 MB, a JPG (PNG and GIF are also accepted for menu items), at least 550 × 440 pixels, ideally 2880 × 2304 in a 5:4 shape. Then try a computer and an up to date browser.

Why was my Uber Eats photo rejected?

Usually text, prices or logos on the image, more than one item, a face in the photo, a blurry or dark photo, or a photo that isn't your real dish.

Can I upload iPhone photos to Uber Eats?

Yes, but iPhones often save photos as HEIC, which isn't on Uber's list of accepted formats. Export or share the photo as a JPG, or set the camera to save in a compatible format.

Why isn't my new photo showing on Uber Eats yet?

Photos are reviewed before they appear, so they can take longer than text changes. Check again later in Uber Eats Manager before uploading it a second time.
