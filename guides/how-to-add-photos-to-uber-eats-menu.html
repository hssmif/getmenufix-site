How to Add Photos to Your Uber Eats Menu (Step by Step)

Photos are added one menu item at a time in Uber Eats Manager. Here are the steps from Uber's own help pages, the rules your photo has to meet, and how to check it before you upload.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

To add a photo to an Uber Eats menu item, open Menu Maker in Uber Eats Manager, click the item, add the photo in the Photo section (drag it in or browse), save, then request approval. Uber reviews each photo before it appears. Use a 5:4 landscape JPG, ideally 2880 × 2304 pixels and under 10 MB.

Before you start: check the photo

Uber's menu photo guidelines ask for a 5:4 landscape photo, ideally 2880 × 2304 pixels (at least 550 × 440), as a JPG under 10 MB, showing one real dish with no text, logos or faces. Our free Uber Eats photo checker checks the size and shape in your browser and can crop it to 5:4 for you. Every rule is in our Uber Eats photo size guide.

How to add a photo to a menu item

These steps follow Uber's help page on adding menu photos:

  1. Sign in to Uber Eats Manager and open Menu Maker.
  2. Go to Overview and click the menu item.
  3. In the Edit item panel, find Photo.
  4. Drag your photo in, or click Browse files and choose it.
  5. Click Save once it has uploaded.
  6. Click Request Approval when Uber asks.

Button names can change a little between versions of Uber Eats Manager. Uber's help page on editing your menu has the current layout.

After you upload

Uber reviews every photo before it appears, so it takes longer than a name or price change. If a photo is turned down, Uber tells you why. The usual reasons are text or logos on the image, more than one item, or a blurry photo.

Photo won't upload?

File too big, the wrong format (like HEIC from an iPhone) or the wrong shape are the common causes. Our guide to Uber Eats photo upload problems goes through each fix.

Which photos to add first

Start with your ten best sellers, then the rest of the menu. Take them in daylight, with the same background and angle for every dish. Our guide to food photos on your phone shows how, and the banner photo guide covers the big photo at the top of your page.

Questions

How do I add photos to my Uber Eats menu?

In Uber Eats Manager, open Menu Maker, click the menu item, add the photo in the Photo section, click Save, then Request Approval. Uber reviews it before it shows.

Why isn't my Uber Eats photo showing yet?

New photos are reviewed by Uber before they appear, so they take longer than a name or price change. Check the item again later.

What size should my Uber Eats photo be?

Uber recommends 2880 × 2304 pixels in a 5:4 landscape shape, at least 550 × 440, as a JPG under 10 MB.

Can I add the same photo to more than one item?

Each photo should show the item it's attached to, exactly as the customer receives it. A combo can show everything that comes in it.
