How to Order Your Uber Eats Menu So Best Sellers Come First

Many customers never scroll far. The order of your menu decides which dishes they see first. Here's how to set it up so your best food is right at the top.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

Put your best sellers first: make the first category a short list of your five or six most ordered dishes, then order the rest of your categories the way people eat (mains, sides, drinks, desserts), and put the most popular dish at the top of each category. Check your sales in Uber Eats Manager and update the order when it changes.

1. Find your real best sellers

Look at your order history in Uber Eats Manager or your own till. Pick the five or six dishes people order most. Use what actually sells, not what you'd like to sell more of.

2. Make a best sellers category and put it first

Create a category called Most Popular, Best Sellers or House Favourites, add those dishes, and drag it to the top. A dish can sit in this category and in its usual one too. Our guide to editing your menu in Uber Eats Manager shows where to do it.

3. Order the other categories the way people eat

  1. Best sellers
  2. Your main dishes, split by type if you have many
  3. Sides and extras
  4. Drinks
  5. Desserts

A café might use Breakfast, Lunch, Coffee, Cold drinks and Cakes instead. More on this in how to organise your Uber Eats menu categories.

4. Put the best item first in every category

The order inside each category matters too. Lead with the most popular or best looking dish, and keep items with photos near the top. A row that starts with a great photo gets looked at.

5. Give the top dishes the best photos and descriptions

Your best sellers are seen the most, so they're where a clear photo and a good description count most. See how to add photos and 30 menu description examples.

6. Check it again every few weeks

Favourites change with the seasons and new dishes. Look at your sales every few weeks and move dishes up or down.

Common mistakes

  • Drinks or extras as the first category.
  • A long first category with every dish in it.
  • Best sellers buried at the bottom of a big category.
  • Old categories from a previous menu still at the top.

Questions

How do I put my best sellers first on Uber Eats?

Create a first category, for example Most Popular, with your five or six most ordered dishes, then drag it to the top of your menu in Uber Eats Manager.

How do I know my best sellers?

Look at your order history and sales in Uber Eats Manager, or your own till. Use the dishes people actually order, not the ones you'd like them to order.

Can a dish be in two categories?

Yes, Uber Eats Manager lets the same item sit in more than one category, so a best seller can be in Most Popular and in its usual place.

How often should I change the order?

Check it every few weeks, and when you add new dishes or change your menu for the season.
