New on Uber Eats? A Checklist for Your First Weeks

The first weeks on Uber Eats are when many people try you for the first time. This checklist helps your page make a strong first impression.

Updated 10 October 2026 · By Abdel Iflillis, founder of MenuFix

Quick answer

If you're new on Uber Eats, before your first orders make sure your best sellers have bright photos, every item has a clear name and short description, your categories are short with best sellers first, your banner shows your best dish, and your hours and prices are correct.

Before your first order

  • Every best seller has a clear, bright photo.
  • Every item has a name people understand and a one line description.
  • Your categories are short and in a sensible order, best sellers first.
  • Your banner shows your most appetising dish.
  • Prices, sizes and what's included in combos are correct.
  • Opening hours match when you can really cook and pack orders.

In your first week

  • Order from yourself once, to see the page and the packaging as a customer does.
  • Check that food travels well: sauces sealed, crispy things not steaming in closed boxes.
  • Pause items as soon as they sell out, so orders aren't cancelled.
  • Read every review and note anything that comes up twice.

In your first month

  • Look at which dishes sell and move them up your menu.
  • Replace weak photos, starting with your top ten items.
  • Remove dishes nobody orders: a shorter menu is easier to choose from.
  • Keep your page consistent with your shop, so regulars recognise you.

A clear page sets the right expectations

When what arrives matches what people saw, they're more likely to be happy and order again. That's the idea behind everything on this list.

Questions

What should I do when I start on Uber Eats?

Add photos to your best sellers, write clear names and descriptions, order your categories with best sellers first, pick an appetising banner and check your hours and prices.

How do I make a good first impression on Uber Eats?

Make sure what customers see matches what arrives: real photos, accurate descriptions and food that travels well.

Should I order from my own restaurant on Uber Eats?

Once is a good idea: you see your page and packaging the way customers do.
