Uber Eats Account Management for Restaurants: Monthly Checklist

Your Uber Eats page is not set and forget. One hour a month on these five things keeps money from leaking and keeps your page easy to choose.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

Once a month, in Uber Eats Manager: reply to new reviews, dispute wrong refunds under Orders within 30 days, check your offers and ads and stop what loses money, update dishes and prices, and keep at least 70% of dishes with a photo and a description, Uber's own goal. 1. Reply to every new review

Short, kind and personal: thank the customer, name the dish, and own any problem without arguing. Our guide on replying to Uber Eats reviews has examples. Then fix the cause, so the same complaint doesn't come back.

2. Dispute wrong refunds and error charges

Open Orders, filter by Shop refunded or Potential deduction, and dispute the wrong ones with a photo of the packed order. Uber's UK help gives you 30 days. See how to dispute Uber Eats error charges.

3. Check your offers and ads

Look at what each offer and ad cost and what it brought. Keep what brings new customers at a price you can afford, and stop what only gives discounts to people who would have ordered anyway.

4. Keep the menu, prices and photos up to date

  • New dishes added, old ones removed, prices checked.
  • Best sellers at the top. See best sellers first.
  • At least 70% of dishes with a photo and a description, Uber's own goal. Use photos of your real food.

5. Look at your numbers

Compare this month with last month: orders, average order, rating, and the dishes people order most. One number moving the wrong way is the thing to fix next month.

Questions

What does Uber Eats account management include?

Replying to reviews, disputing wrong refunds and error charges, checking offers and ads, keeping the menu, prices and photos up to date, and watching your ratings and sales each month.

Can someone manage my Uber Eats account for me?

Yes. In Uber Eats Manager you can add a user with manager access, so they can work on your page with their own login. Never share your own password.

How often should I check my Uber Eats account?

Look at refunds once a week, because disputes have a time limit, and do the full checklist once a month.
