How to Dispute Uber Eats Error Charges and Refunds (2026)

When a customer says an item was missing or wrong, the refund can come out of your payout. Many of those charges can be disputed, but only if you find them in time and have proof.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

In Uber Eats Manager, open Orders and filter by Shop refunded or Potential deduction. Choose the order, select Dispute, give the reason and add a photo or video as proof. What an error charge is

If a customer reports a missing item, a wrong item, a wrong order or a problem with the food, Uber may refund them and take that cost from your payout. Uber calls these order error adjustments. One or two a week can quietly add up to a lot of money over a year.

Where to find them in Uber Eats Manager

  • Open Orders in Uber Eats Manager.
  • Filter by Shop refunded or Potential deduction.
  • Open each order to see what the customer reported, and any photo or comment they added.

Make it a habit: look once a week, so nothing gets older than the time limit.

How to dispute, step by step

  1. Choose the order and select Dispute.
  2. Pick the reason that fits, in plain words: for example, the drink was in the bag and the bag was sealed.
  3. Add your proof: a photo or short video of the packed order.
  4. Send it, then follow it under Refund disputed.

Uber decides case by case. If a dispute is refused and you still think it is wrong, contact support from Uber Eats Manager.

The 30 day limit, and who can dispute

Uber's UK help page on order errors says disputes must be filed within 30 days of the order, and only admins and manager level users can submit them. Rules can differ by country, so check the help page for yours.

Proof that helps

  • A photo of every bag before pickup, sealed, with the receipt showing. It takes five seconds.
  • Sealed bags with a sticker, so nobody can say something fell out.
  • Drinks and sides checked against the ticket, because they are the items most often reported missing.

Uber says it now also looks at delivery photos, timing and courier reports when it decides an order error, and customers can add photos to their claim. Your own photo is often what makes the difference.

How to stop them happening

  • Read every ticket twice at packing, especially options and extras.
  • Pause items the moment they sell out, so nobody orders what you can't send.
  • Use photos of your real food. When the dish that arrives looks like the photo, fewer customers complain that it was "not as pictured".
  • Write clear options: size, number of pieces, what comes in a combo.

Questions

What is an Uber Eats error charge?

When a customer reports a missing item, a wrong item, a wrong order or a food quality problem and gets a refund, Uber can take that refund from your payout. This is often called an error charge or an order error adjustment.

How long do I have to dispute an Uber Eats refund?

Uber's UK merchant help says 30 days from the order. Check the help page for your country, and dispute as soon as you see the charge.

Who can dispute an order error on Uber Eats?

Only users with admin or manager access in Uber Eats Manager. If someone helps you with your account, add them as a user with their own login. Never share your password.

What proof helps an Uber Eats dispute?

A photo or short video of the order packed and sealed before pickup, with the receipt visible. Uber says it now also looks at delivery photos, timing and courier reports when it decides.

Am I charged for every refund on Uber Eats?

No. Uber's UK help says restaurants are not charged for fraud, for Uber's own goodwill credits, or for delivery and bag fees. Check the help page for your country.
