How to Get Your Restaurant on Uber Eats in Australia

Joining Uber Eats is mostly paperwork and setting up your menu. Here are the steps for an Australian restaurant or café, what to have ready, what it costs, and what to do in your first week.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

To get your restaurant on Uber Eats in Australia, sign up on Uber's merchant site, give your business details and menu, and wait for Uber to approve your location. Uber lists a one off A$700 activation fee (excl. GST) for a new location, taken from your payouts. Then set up your menu, photos and hours in Uber Eats Manager before your first orders.

The sign up steps

  1. Start on Uber's merchant site. Go to the Uber Eats sign up page and enter your business name, address and contact details.
  2. Give Uber what it asks for. Uber's merchant terms say your location is added only after you provide the documents and information Uber requests and Uber approves it.
  3. Set up your menu. Add your dishes, prices, categories and opening hours.
  4. Add photos. Start with your best sellers. See how to add photos to your Uber Eats menu.
  5. Go live. Once Uber approves your location, you start taking orders.

What to have ready

Uber's help page on documents for sign up lists:

  • an up to date copy of your menu, with prices and descriptions;
  • your opening hours;
  • a business or food licence.

Requirements can differ by country, and Uber tells you exactly what it needs for your location during sign up. Have your ABN and bank details handy too, so you can finish without stopping.

What it costs

Uber's pricing page lists a one off A$700 activation fee (excl. GST) for a new location, taken from your payouts in weekly instalments, plus a percentage of each order. Our guide to Uber Eats fees for restaurants in Australia explains each rate and how to work out what you keep. Check your own agreement too: it's what counts.

Set your prices before you go live

Commission comes out of every order, so a price that works at your counter can lose money on delivery. Work it out dish by dish with our guide on how to price your menu on Uber Eats.

Your first week

  • Best sellers first, each with a clear photo of your real food.
  • A short description for every dish. Check yours with our free menu description checker.
  • Hours that match when you can really cook and pack orders.
  • Order once from yourself, to see your page and packaging the way customers do.

Questions

How do I get my restaurant on Uber Eats in Australia?

Sign up on Uber's merchant site, give your business and location details, upload your menu, then wait for Uber to approve your location. After that you set everything up in Uber Eats Manager.

How much does it cost to join Uber Eats as a restaurant?

Uber's pricing page lists a one off A$700 activation fee (excl. GST) for a new location, taken from your payouts in weekly instalments, plus a percentage of each order. Your own agreement is what counts.

What do I need to sign up for Uber Eats?

Uber asks for documents and information during sign up. Its help page lists a menu with prices and descriptions, your opening hours and a business or food licence. Uber tells you exactly what it needs for your location.

How long until I get my first order?

That depends on Uber's approval and how quickly you set up your menu. A complete page with photos and clear names gives you the best start.
