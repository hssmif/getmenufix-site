Uber Eats Food Photography Cost: Photographer, Uber's Photoshoot or Your Phone

Good photos are one of the easiest ways to make your Uber Eats page more appetising. There are three ways to get them, and they cost very different amounts. Here's what each involves, with no made up prices.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

Uber Eats food photography can cost nothing or a fair amount, depending on the route. Uber has its own photography session for restaurants (ask Uber Eats support if you haven't had one), a local food photographer charges what they quote, and enhancing photos you take on your phone costs the least. Prices for photographers vary, so get two or three quotes.

Option 1: Uber's own photography session

Uber has a help page on how restaurants schedule photography. It says restaurants that have partnered with Uber Eats but haven't had their photography session can contact support to arrange one. What's included, and whether there's a cost, depends on your agreement, so check Uber's pricing page and ask support.

  • Good: arranged by Uber, and made for the Uber Eats app.
  • Keep in mind: you work around Uber's schedule, and new dishes later may need new photos.

Option 2: Hire a food photographer

A local food photographer comes to you, styles the dishes and edits the photos. Prices vary a lot by city, the number of dishes and what's included, so there's no single price to quote. Ask two or three photographers for a written quote, and check each one includes:

  • A price for your number of dishes, not per hour only.
  • Edited files sized for Uber Eats: 2880 × 2304 pixels, 5:4 (see the Uber Eats photo size guide).
  • The right to use the photos on every delivery app and your own social media.
  • When you get the files.

This suits restaurants that want a consistent style across the whole menu and have the budget for it.

Option 3: Take photos on your phone, then enhance them

A recent phone, daylight from a window and a clean background get you most of the way. Our guide to taking food photos on your phone walks you through it. You can then improve the light, colour and background yourself, or have it done, as long as the photo still shows your real dish.

If you'd rather not edit them yourself, our Menu and Photos package enhances your real photos of up to 15 dishes and delivers them sized for Uber Eats.

What if a dish has no photo at all?

Uber Eats can show photos taken by customers on items that have no photo from the restaurant (Uber). Adding your own photos to your best sellers means customers see the dish the way you serve it.

Which option is right for you?

  • Just joined Uber Eats: ask support about Uber's photography session first.
  • Want a premium look across a big menu: get quotes from photographers.
  • Want to start this week with little cost: phone photos, enhanced.

Whichever you choose, start with your ten best sellers. Our guide on getting more orders explains why.

Questions

How much does Uber Eats food photography cost?

It depends on the route. Uber's own photography session is arranged through Uber, a local photographer charges what they quote for your dishes, and phone photos you enhance yourself cost the least. Photographer prices vary by city and number of dishes, so get a few quotes.

Does Uber Eats do a photoshoot for restaurants?

Uber has a help page on how restaurants schedule their photography session. If you joined and haven't had yours, it says to contact Uber Eats support to arrange it. Check your own agreement for what's included.

Can I use my own photos on Uber Eats?

Yes. You can upload your own photos in Uber Eats Manager, as long as they follow Uber's photo rules: your real dish, one item, no text or logos, the right size.

What should I ask a food photographer?

Ask for a price for your number of dishes, edited files at 2880 × 2304 pixels, how soon you get them, and whether you can use the photos on every delivery app.
