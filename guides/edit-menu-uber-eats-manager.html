Uber Eats Manager: How to Edit Your Menu, Photos and Categories

How to update my menu on Uber Eats is one of the most common questions from restaurant owners. It's all done in Uber Eats Manager. Here's where to find each setting, step by step. Screens change from time to time, but the main steps stay the same.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

To edit your Uber Eats menu, sign in at merchants.ubereats.com, open Menu, choose your delivery menu, click an item to change its name, description or photo, and save. To let someone help, add them as a user instead of sharing your password.

How to update my menu on Uber Eats: the short version

  1. Sign in to Uber Eats Manager on a computer.
  2. Open Menu and choose the menu you use for delivery.
  3. Click the item, category or photo you want to change.
  4. Make the change, then Save or Publish.

The steps below go through each part in more detail.

Sign in to Uber Eats Manager

Go to merchants.ubereats.com on a computer and sign in with your restaurant account. The menu editor is easier to use on a computer than on a phone.

Change an item's name or description

  1. Click Menu in the left side menu.
  2. Open the menu you use for delivery.
  3. Click the item you want to change.
  4. Edit the name and description, then click Save.

Add or replace a photo

If a photo won't upload, see Uber Eats photo upload not working.

  1. Open the item in the menu editor.
  2. Click the photo area.
  3. Upload your new photo. Use a landscape photo with the dish in the centre.
  4. Save, and check how it looks on the app once it's live.

Reorder categories and items

In the menu editor you can drag categories and items into a new order. Put your best sellers and most popular category first.

Publish your changes

Depending on your setup, changes may save straight away or need you to click Publish. They usually show on the app within a few minutes.

Let someone help, without sharing your password

If someone helps you with your page, don't give them your password. Instead, add them as a user:

  1. In Uber Eats Manager, open Settings, then Users (sometimes called Team).
  2. Add a new user with their email address.
  3. Give access to the Menu only.

Questions

How to update my menu on Uber Eats?

Sign in to Uber Eats Manager at merchants.ubereats.com, open Menu, choose the menu you use for delivery, click the item, change it and save or publish. Text changes usually show within minutes.

How do I change my menu on Uber Eats?

Sign in to Uber Eats Manager at merchants.ubereats.com, open Menu, click the item and edit it, then save or publish.

How long do Uber Eats menu changes take?

Text changes usually show within minutes. New photos are reviewed first, which can take longer.

How do I give someone access to my Uber Eats Manager?

In Uber Eats Manager open Settings, then Users, add their email and give access to the Menu only. You can remove it any time.
