How to Improve Your Uber Eats Rating (for Restaurants)

Your rating comes from what happens after someone orders. You can't control the traffic or the courier, but a lot of it is in your hands. Here's what restaurants can do, without promises.

Updated 10 October 2026 · By Abdel Iflillis, founder of MenuFix

Quick answer

To improve your Uber Eats rating as a restaurant, make sure what arrives matches what customers saw: accurate photos and descriptions, packaging that keeps food hot and crisp, sold out items switched off, and orders checked before handoff. Read your feedback in Uber Eats Manager and fix what comes up more than once. No one can promise a rating, but these are the parts you control.

How Uber Eats ratings work

After their order arrives, customers rate it from 1 to 5 stars. Uber's ratings help page says your store rating is based on recent ratings, so changes you make show up over time. You can see your rating, ratings for each item and written feedback in Uber Eats Manager (where to find them).

1. Make your page match what arrives

Most low ratings come from a gap between what people expected and what turned up. Use photos of your real dishes in real portions, and descriptions that say what's in the dish and how big it is. Our guide to writing menu descriptions helps, and the description checker flags vague wording.

2. Pack food so it travels

  • Vented boxes for chips and anything crumbed, so they don't go soggy.
  • Sauces and dressings in sealed pots on the side.
  • Hot and cold items packed apart, with ice cream or salads in their own container.
  • A sealed bag, so nothing spills on the way.

3. Switch off sold out items straight away

A missing item means a changed or cancelled order, and an unhappy customer. Pause items in Uber Eats Manager the moment you run out, and switch them back on when you can make them.

4. Check every order before it leaves

Uber's ratings help page suggests checking each order before handoff. A quick look at the ticket against the bag catches missing drinks, sides and sauces, which are an easy cause of bad ratings.

5. Read your feedback and fix what repeats

One comment is one person's view. The same comment twice is worth acting on. If it's about the food, look at the recipe or portion. If it's about expectations, fix the photo or description. See our guide on replying to Uber Eats reviews, with examples.

6. Keep your hours honest

Accept orders only when you can cook and pack them properly. Busy times are when mistakes happen, so if the kitchen is overloaded, it's better to pause for a while than send out rushed food.

What you can't control

Traffic, the courier and the weather affect some ratings, and that's frustrating. Focus on what you can change: a clear, accurate page and food that travels well.

Questions

How do I improve my restaurant's rating on Uber Eats?

Make what arrives match your page: accurate photos and descriptions, good packaging, sold out items switched off and orders checked before they leave. Then read your feedback in Uber Eats Manager and fix what repeats.

How are Uber Eats restaurant ratings worked out?

Customers rate their order from 1 to 5 stars after it arrives. Uber's ratings help page says your store rating is based on recent ratings, so improvements show over time.

Where can I see my Uber Eats reviews?

In Uber Eats Manager. Uber's help page explains where to see your store rating, ratings for each item and written feedback.

Can I remove a bad rating on Uber Eats?

Generally no. Focus on fixing the cause. If a review breaks Uber's rules, you can report it through Uber Eats Manager or support.
