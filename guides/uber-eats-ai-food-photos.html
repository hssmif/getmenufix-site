Uber Eats AI Food Photos: What Restaurants Should Know (2026)

Uber Eats can now change how your dishes look: AI can improve a weak photo, and customers' photos can appear on items with no picture. Here's what that means, and how to keep control of your own page.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

Since July 2025 Uber Eats can use AI to improve low quality food photos, for example better light or a dish moved onto a plate, and it can show photos taken by customers on items that have no photo. Uber's own guidance says a photo should stay a true representation of the dish. The safest choice is clear photos of your real food on every popular item, so customers get what they saw.

What Uber Eats does with AI

In July 2025 Uber announced new AI tools for restaurants, reported by TechCrunch: AI that improves low quality food photos, AI menu descriptions you can approve, and AI summaries of reviews. Uber also pays customers in some countries a small credit when their photo of a dish is chosen and published.

Customer photos on dishes without a picture

If an item has no photo from you, Uber can show a photo from a verified recent customer, after checking it against its guidelines. That photo may be taken in poor light or show the dish after the trip. To control what people see, upload your own photo of every popular dish in Menu Maker.

Why photos of your real food still win

  • Customers get what they saw. When the dish that arrives matches the photo, there are fewer "not as pictured" complaints, refunds and low ratings.
  • The rules point the same way. Uber's photo guidance asks for a true representation of the dish, and DoorDash's rules for virtual brands ban images that are evidently made by AI.
  • People notice. In 2026 several restaurants faced public anger online for AI food pictures. Real photos build trust.

Uber's own goal: 70% of your dishes

In April 2026 Uber Eats said customers are more likely to order when at least 70% of a menu's items have a photo and a description, and that stores which reached it in their first week sold more on average (Uber Eats, Get discovered; Uber notes results may vary). Count your own menu: how many dishes have both?

How to get there with your own photos

  • Shoot your ten best sellers first, in daylight, on a plain plate. Our guide to food photos on your phone shows how.
  • Check Uber's sizes in our photo requirements guide.
  • Add a one line description to every dish with a photo.

Questions

Does Uber Eats use AI on food photos?

Yes. In July 2025 Uber announced it would use AI to improve low quality food images, for example their light, framing or plating. A dish shown in a takeaway box can become a picture of the same dish on a plate.

Why are there customer photos on my Uber Eats menu?

When an item has no photo from the restaurant, Uber can use a photo from a verified recent customer after checking it. Upload your own photo of the dish in Menu Maker to replace it.

Can I use AI generated food photos on Uber Eats?

Uber's photo guidance asks for a true representation of the dish, and DoorDash's rules for virtual brands ban images that are evidently made by AI. If the food that arrives doesn't match the picture, customers complain. Use photos of your real dishes.

How many dishes need a photo on Uber Eats?

Uber says customers are more likely to order when at least 70% of the items on a menu have a photo and a description. Start with your best sellers.
