Moving from Menulog to Uber Eats: Set Up Your Page Right

A new Uber Eats page starts from zero: no ratings, no regulars on the app yet. The first weeks decide who tries you, so it pays to set the page up properly instead of copying your old menu across as it is. Every requirement below comes from Uber's own merchant pages.

Updated 10 October 2026

Quick answer

Sign up on Uber's merchant site with an up to date menu (prices and descriptions), your opening hours and your business or food licence. Uber adds your location once it approves what you sent. Then set up the page properly: best sellers first, clear names, a short description for every dish, and photos that meet Uber's rules: 5:4 landscape, ideally 2880 × 2304 pixels, JPG up to 10 MB, one dish, no text or logos.

1. Sign up with what Uber asks for

Start on the Uber Eats sign up page with your business name, address and contact details. Uber's help page on documents for sign up lists what to have ready:

  • an up to date copy of your menu, with prices and descriptions;
  • your opening hours;
  • a business or food licence.

Uber's merchant terms say your location is added only after you provide the documents and information Uber requests and Uber approves it. Have everything ready before you start, so you can finish in one go.

2. Don't copy your old menu across as it is

Your menu is the part of your new page you control completely, and a fresh page is the easiest moment to get it right:

  • Best sellers first. Put the five or six dishes people order most in your first category.
  • Names people understand. Keep your signature names, and add what the dish is.
  • A short description for every dish. One line with the main ingredients is enough. Our guide to menu descriptions has examples.
  • Short, logical categories. Group the menu the way people order. See how to set up your categories.
  • Correct prices, sizes and options. Build combos and add ons carefully and place a test order if you can.

3. Photos that pass Uber's review

Uber reviews every photo before it goes live. Its menu photo guidelines ask for:

WhatUber's requirement
Shape5:4, landscape
Recommended size2880 × 2304 pixels
Minimum size550 pixels wide, 440 pixels tall
FileJPG (PNG and GIF also accepted for menu items), up to 10 MB
ContentOne menu item, as served, centred. No text, prices, logos, watermarks or faces (hands are fine)

Photos made for another app can be a different shape. Crop again from your original photos to 5:4 rather than stretching an old crop. Start with your best sellers. Our free photo checker tells you if a photo has the right size and shape.

4. A cover image that shows your food

Uber's guide to cover images and menu photos asks for 2880 × 2304 pixels, a 5:4 shape, as a JPG, filling the whole size, showing food customers can actually buy, with no logos or text apart from your store name. Our guide to choosing your banner photo helps you pick the dish.

5. Plan your first weeks

A new page has no ratings yet, so what people see on it does the work. Our first weeks checklist covers the rest: hours that match when you can really cook, pausing sold out items, and reading every review.

6. Fees: check your own agreement

Uber's pricing page lists its current fees. What you actually pay is in your own agreement, so check it there, including any special terms Uber has offered you.

Sources

Everything above about Uber Eats comes from Uber's own merchant pages: the sign up page, documents for sign up, the merchant terms, the menu photo guidelines, cover images and menu photos and the pricing page. Uber can change them, so check those pages if something doesn't match.

Questions

Can I copy my Menulog menu to Uber Eats?

Use it as a starting point. Uber asks for an up to date menu with prices and descriptions when you sign up. Check every name, price and option as you enter it, and put your best sellers first.

Can I use my Menulog photos on Uber Eats?

Only if they meet Uber's photo guidelines: 5:4 landscape, ideally 2880 × 2304 pixels, at least 550 × 440, one dish as served, no text, logos or faces. Crop again from your original photos rather than reusing an already cropped version.

When will my Uber Eats page go live?

Uber's merchant terms say your location is added once you have given Uber the documents and information it asks for and Uber approves it. Having your menu, opening hours and licence ready avoids delays.

What does Uber Eats cost?

Uber's pricing page lists its current fees, and your own agreement is what counts. If Uber has offered you special terms, check them in your agreement.
