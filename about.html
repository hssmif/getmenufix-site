Our story

Small places, real food.

Some of the best food in any city comes from small, independent kitchens. On delivery apps, though, they often look worse than the big chains: no photos, menus typed in a hurry, best dishes hidden at the bottom.

MenuFix is a small independent service run by its founder, Abdel Iflillis. We do one thing, delivery app pages, so we know exactly what helps people choose you. No agency fees and no long contracts. You deal with us directly, by email.
