Delivery driver side hustle: questions

How can delivery drivers make extra money?

Introduce restaurants you already know to MenuFix. Show them your free QR card on your own time; the owner checks their Uber Eats page for free. If they buy a makeover, you earn a 20% commission. It is a side hustle that fits around your delivery shifts.

Do I need to sell anything?

No. You show your card to restaurants you know, at a quiet moment. The owner scans it and checks their own Uber Eats page with us for free. If they buy a makeover later, you earn.

How much can I earn per referral?

20% of what the restaurant pays us. The Uber Eats Makeover is around 970 Australian dollars, so about 190 dollars for you from one restaurant, and more for the bigger packages. Refunded payments don't count.

When do I get paid?

Within 7 days after the restaurant's payment, by bank transfer or PayPal.

Can Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo and Menulog riders join?

Yes. Any delivery rider or driver can join: Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo, Menulog, Just Eat or a cycling courier. MenuFix is independent and not linked to any delivery app.

Can this cause problems with my rider account?

Follow the rules on this page: never during a delivery, never in delivery gear, never say you are from Uber, never use the app or contact customers. You talk to restaurants on your own time, as yourself.

Which cities?

Anywhere restaurants sell on Uber Eats: Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, London, Manchester, Dublin, New York and more, in Australia, the UK, Ireland, the USA and New Zealand.
