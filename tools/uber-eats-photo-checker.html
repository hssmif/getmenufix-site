Free Uber Eats photo checker.

Check a dish photo against Uber's size rules before you upload it, and fix the shape in one click.

What this checks

  • Shape: close to 5:4 landscape, the shape Uber recommends.
  • Size: at least 550 × 440 pixels; 2880 × 2304 is ideal.
  • File size: under 10 MB.
  • File type: JPG or PNG.

These come from Uber's menu photo guidelines. The tool can't check what's in the photo: Uber also turns down photos with text, logos, more than one item or faces. See photo upload not working for every reason and fix.

The fix button crops the middle of your photo to 5:4 and saves a JPG at 2880 × 2304. If your photo is smaller than that, it gets bigger but not sharper, so a larger original is always better.
