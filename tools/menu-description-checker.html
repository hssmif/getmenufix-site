What this checks

  • Length: Uber's advice is 140 to 260 characters, about two short sentences (Uber Australia).
  • ALL CAPS: words in capitals read like shouting and are harder to skim.
  • Vague words: delicious, amazing, best and similar say nothing about the dish.
  • Ingredients: whether it names at least two foods or cooking methods.
