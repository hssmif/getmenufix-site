What we do

MenuFix improves the content of your Uber Eats page: item names, descriptions, the order of your menu and photos of your real dishes. What each package includes is described on our home page and at checkout.

Free mockup

The free mockup is a concept preview to show you the idea. It is not a final design and it is never used on your page without your agreement.

Payment

Prices are one time prices shown at checkout, in your currency. Payment is taken securely by Stripe when you order.

What we need from you

  • For packages with photos: photos of your real dishes, sent by email.
  • For Done for you: access to your Uber Eats Manager as a user. You can remove our access any time.

Delivery

We usually deliver within two to three working days after we have what we need from you. Do it yourself: we email you everything ready to copy, with a guide. Done for you: we make the changes on your Uber Eats page and let you know when it's done.

Your content

Your menu, your photos and the new texts we write for you are yours to use on Uber Eats and anywhere else. We only enhance photos of your real food and never add dishes or ingredients that aren't on your menu.

No guarantees on results

We make your page clearer and more appetising, but we can't promise a specific number of orders, ratings or sales, because these depend on many things outside our control.

Questions or problems

If something isn't right, email us and we'll do our best to fix it with you.
