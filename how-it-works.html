How it works

Four simple steps.

Everything can be done by email. You see the idea before you pay anything.

Step 1

Your free mockup

Tell us your restaurant or café on Uber Eats. We look at your page and send you a mockup of how it could look, with the main things we'd change. No payment, no obligation.

Step 2

Choose your package

Pick Menu Fix or Menu and Photos to do it yourself, or the Full Makeover and we make every change for you. Pay securely by card through Stripe. One time price, no subscription.

Step 3

Send your photos

For photo packages, take photos of your dishes on your phone, in good light, and reply to our email with them. We enhance your real photos. We never use fake food.
