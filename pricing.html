Pricing questions

How long does it take?

Usually two to three working days after we have your photos and access to your Uber Eats Manager. A website takes a few days more.

What do I need to send you?

Photos of your dishes taken on your phone in good light (near a window is perfect), and access as a user to your Uber Eats Manager. We do everything else.

What if I don't like it?

You don't pay. We make your finished page first and send it to you, before and after, with every change listed. If you're happy, you pay. If not, tell us what to change, or simply say no and you owe nothing.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe, a secure payment provider, once you've seen your finished page and you're happy with it. You pay in the currency shown on this page.

Do I need to give you my password?

No. You add us as a user on your Uber Eats Manager, and you can remove us any time. We never ask for your password.
