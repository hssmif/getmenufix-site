What usually costs you orders

Most small places lose customers on things that are quick to fix. These are the ones we see again and again.

📷

Missing or dark photos

People scroll past dishes they can't see. Your best items need a clear, bright photo of the real food.

✏️

Typos and unclear names

"Chiken", "mined beef" or names only regulars understand make a menu look careless.

💬

Empty descriptions

A short line that says what's in the dish and why it's good helps people decide, especially at higher prices.

⬆️

Best sellers buried

Your most popular dishes should be the first thing people see, not hidden at the bottom.

🗂️

Messy categories

Too many sections, or the wrong order, make it hard to find what to order.

🆕

New on Uber Eats

No ratings yet? Your page is all customers have to go on. First impressions matter most now.

About MenuFix

MenuFix is a small independent service run by Sam. We work only on delivery app pages for independent restaurants, cafés and small shops, so we know exactly what helps people choose you. No agency fees, no long contracts, and you can reach us directly by email.

Questions

Do I need to give you my password?

No. For Done for you, you add us as a user on your Uber Eats Manager. You can remove us any time.

Do you use fake food photos?

Never. We only enhance photos of your real dishes: better light, a clean background and the right size. Uber Eats photos must show your real food, and so do ours. The free mockup is a concept preview only.

How long does it take?

Usually two to three working days after we have your photos.

Will this guarantee more orders?

No one can honestly promise that. What we do is make your page clearer and more appetising, which is what helps people choose you.

Is MenuFix part of Uber?

No. MenuFix is independent and not affiliated with Uber or Uber Eats.

Which countries do you work with?

Restaurants and shops on Uber Eats in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Ireland and more. Prices show in your currency at checkout.
